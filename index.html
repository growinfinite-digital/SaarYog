About SaarYog

Yoga for me, myself.

SaarYog is a yoga and yoga-therapy centre in Pune led by doctors who are also yoga teachers. Every batch blends asanas, pranayam, meditation and cleansing kriyas with simple knowledge sessions so you learn to care for your own health.

  • Dr. Yogeshwar Sanap, MD – Yog Master (YCB 2, 3, 4), Yog Teacher & Evaluator, Therapeutic Yog Consultant, Consulting Family Physician.
  • Dr. Rekha Sanap, MD – Consulting Homoeopath, Yog Teacher, Happiness Program Teacher (Art of Living).
  • Mrs. Surekha Sawant – Yog Teacher.
