About SaarYog

Yoga for me, myself.

SaarYog is a yoga and yoga-therapy centre in Pune led by doctors who are also yoga teachers. Every batch blends asanas, pranayam, meditation and cleansing kriyas with simple knowledge sessions so you learn to care for your own health.
